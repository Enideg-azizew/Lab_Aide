Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 4, 2026

Educational Notice: This is a learning project. No real patient data is being collected or stored.

1. Introduction

At MediLab Clinical Laboratory ("we", "our", "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our laboratory management system.

Note: This is a learning project created for educational purposes. All data entered is for demonstration only.

2. Information We Collect

In this educational demo, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Username, email address, and password (for demo accounts)
  • Demographic Data: Sample patient information for testing purposes
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the application features
  • Laboratory Data: Test results, SOPs, and inventory management data
Important: All data is stored locally in SQLite and reset periodically. Do not enter real patient information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Demonstrating laboratory management features
  • Educational purposes and training
  • Testing and improving the application
  • Showcasing functionality like SOP management, inventory tracking, and result reporting

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Django's built-in security features
  • Password hashing and encryption
  • CSRF protection
  • SQLite database with proper permissions

However, as this is a learning project, we recommend not storing sensitive or real data.

5. Data Retention

Since this is a demo application:

  • Data is retained until the database is reset
  • No long-term storage of any information
  • Database can be cleared at any time by administrators

6. Your Rights

For this learning project, you have the right to:

  • Access your demo account data
  • Delete your test data
  • Request information about collected data
  • Opt-out of the demonstration at any time
